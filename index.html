About Me

I care about game design and learning.

How is the onboarding experience? Is the tutorial boring? Does the player/learner have the tools they need to clear the next challenge? What system(s) can we add/remove to make the player/learner feel that thing? I think a lot about onboarding, tutorialization, systems thinking, player psychology, and player led storytelling, among other topics. I approach every project with lots of questions, a desire to understand how design intentions and goals are aligning with how players will learn about systems, engage with mechanics and eventually create their own meaningful and empowering experiences.

I also have experience as an educator teaching both adults in corporate training environments and public school students in traditional classrooms and educational institutions. I've witnessed a lot of parellels between good learning design and good game design and I think my experiences in each continue to develop my skills in the other. Outside of learning/game design, I also mix music in between freetime. I've been told my understanding for building/blending rhythm, tension, and release directly parallels the best kinds of game/learning design that utilizes onboarding tutorials, challenge pacing and feedback loops.

I'm currently working as a product & game design consultant with different EdTech startups, but I'm open to opportunities in learning/game design, UX, game education or creative strategy roles where I can bring game design thinking & analysis with a sensitivity to learner psychology & human experience to help create meaningful learning experiences for people.
